How we estimate this
  • Water: EPA WaterSense says the average family wastes 10,000+ gallons a year to leaks. We assume half of users have one and fix it.
  • Energy: household feedback programs cut electricity use 4–12% (ACEEE meta-review of 57 programs). We use the low end, 4% of ~10,500 kWh a year.
  • Food: ReFED puts consumer food waste at about $800 per person a year. We assume a 25% cut for ~2.3 people per home.
  • Households: ~101,000 in Richmond (Community Climate Collaborative, 2025). These are estimates, not measured results.

Wattson’s AI

Wattson reads bills and answers questions with an AI model that runs entirely on this laptop. Nothing you scan is uploaded anywhere.

To turn it on:
  1. Install and open Ollama (ollama.com).
  2. In the project folder, run ollama create wattson -f demo/Modelfile
  3. Click Check again.
